PPhandcontrol · Quick start

Your hand.
Your next slide.

Open one palm and swipe to control your presentation or PDF. Everything runs on your computer.

Windows 10/11 x64No Python installationWorks offline
01 · EXTRACT & OPEN

A folder, not an installer

  1. Download PPhandcontrol-windows-x64.zip from the official repository's Releases page.
  2. Right-click → Extract All. Keep every extracted file and folder together.
  3. Open PPhandcontrol.exe. It starts paused.
  4. Select your camera and leave sensitivity at Medium for your first try.
02 · PREPARE THE VIEWER

Give your slides the focus

  1. Select the matching navigation profile using the table below.
  2. Open your slides or PDF. Start slideshow, fullscreen, or single-page mode.
  3. Click the slide/page area, outside menus and text boxes.
  4. Press Ctrl + Alt + P to resume without switching windows.
03 · MAKE A GESTURE

Open. Hold. Swipe.

  1. Face one open palm toward the camera with every finger visible. Hold it still briefly.
  2. Move left in the mirrored preview for Next; move right for Previous.
  3. Wait for the 0.8-second cooldown, then hold still to rearm.
  4. Close your palm while moving back to the starting position; open and hold still before the next swipe.
04 · STAY IN CONTROL

Pause whenever you need

Ctrl + Alt + P pauses/resumes anywhere.
Ctrl + Alt + Q quits anywhere.

P and Q also work while the app window has focus, alongside its buttons.

Minimize the app after setup. Tracking continues, and your presentation keeps focus.

Directions follow the mirrored preview. Enable Reverse directions if you prefer the opposite mapping. Navigation is suppressed while PPhandcontrol has focus; click your viewer before testing.

Choose the right profile

ProfileKeyboard commandsPrepare the viewer
PowerPoint / Google SlidesRight / Left arrowStart Slideshow and focus the slide. PowerPoint advances normal animation steps.
Adobe Acrobat / ReaderCtrl+PageDown / Ctrl+PageUpFocus the PDF page. Fullscreen helps.
SumatraPDFPageDown / PageUpUse single-page or presentation mode and focus the document.

Keys go to whichever application has focus. Browser PDF viewers are outside guaranteed first-release compatibility. A viewer running as administrator can block incoming keys.

Make tracking reliable

  • Use even lighting and show the whole hand.
  • Keep one open palm in view and swipe mostly horizontally.
  • Choose Low sensitivity for a larger required swipe, High for a smaller one.
  • Pause during unrelated hand movements.
  • Tracking loss clears old motion. Hold still again after re-entry.

If something stops working

  • No camera: check Windows camera privacy settings, close video-call apps, or choose another camera.
  • No slide change: focus the viewer's page area and check the profile.
  • No global shortcut: another program may already own it. Use the app buttons or focused P/Q controls.
  • Camera disconnected: navigation pauses. Reconnect/retry, then resume.
