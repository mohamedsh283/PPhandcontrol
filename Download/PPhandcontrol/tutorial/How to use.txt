PPhandcontrol 1.1.0 - How to use
Created by Mohamed Sherif | Windows 10/11 x64

START
1. Close the old version if it is running. Extract the new ZIP to a fresh folder.
2. Keep the entire folder, including _internal. Open PPhandcontrol.exe.
3. The app starts PAUSED. Choose your viewer profile and camera (usually 0).
4. Leave Gesture mode on THUMBS, the default, or select POINT & SWIPE.
5. Open your slideshow or PDF and click its page. Press Ctrl+Alt+P to resume.

MODE 1: THUMBS (DEFAULT)
THUMBS UP = NEXT slide, animation step, or PDF page.
THUMBS DOWN = PREVIOUS slide, animation step, or PDF page.
Keep the other fingers folded, show one hand clearly, and hold the pose briefly
(about 0.35 seconds on Medium sensitivity). No horizontal swipe is needed.
A held thumb sends one command. Relax/lower it for about 0.25 seconds before
repeating. A stable opposite thumb can change direction after the cooldown.

MODE 2: POINT & SWIPE (OPTIONAL)
Extend your INDEX FINGER toward the camera, with the other fingers folded.
Swipe your pointing HAND LEFT in the mirrored preview = NEXT.
Swipe your pointing HAND RIGHT = PREVIOUS.
Keep the whole hand visible; use a slight angle if the fingertip hides it.
Relax/retract the finger after each command and while returning your hand.
An open palm does not trigger this mode. There is no old open-palm hold step.

CONTROLS
P = pause/resume in the app. Q = quit in the app.
Ctrl+Alt+P = pause/resume globally. Ctrl+Alt+Q = quit globally.
The app works while minimized. Click your viewer before gesturing; navigation
is suppressed when PPhandcontrol itself is focused.
Changing mode/settings pauses gestures. Resume when ready.
High sensitivity = shorter thumb hold / smaller pointing swipe.
Low sensitivity = longer thumb hold / larger pointing swipe.
There is a 0.8-second cooldown between commands.

VIEWERS
PowerPoint / Google Slides: normal next/previous animation or slide step.
Adobe Acrobat / Reader: next/previous PDF page.
SumatraPDF: next/previous PDF page; single-page/presentation mode recommended.
Commands go to the focused window. Browser PDF viewers are not guaranteed.

TROUBLESHOOTING
No camera: close video-call/camera apps, check camera permissions, select a
different camera index, or click Retry camera.
Missed gesture: use even lighting, keep one whole hand visible, check the mode
and recognition status, and try High sensitivity. Relax between repeated poses.
No navigation: resume gestures, choose the right profile, and click the page.
Quit the OLD app before opening the new app so it releases camera and shortcuts.
If a shortcut conflicts, use the app's P/Q keys or buttons.
The EXE is unsigned. If device policy blocks it, contact the administrator or
use the repository's source instructions. Do not remove _internal.
