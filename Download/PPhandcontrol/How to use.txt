PPhandcontrol - How to use
Created by Mohamed Sherif
Windows 10/11, 64-bit | Version 1.0.0

START
1. Keep the whole PPhandcontrol folder together, including _internal.
2. Double-click PPhandcontrol.exe. You do not need to install Python.
3. Allow camera access if Windows asks. The app starts PAUSED.
4. Choose the presentation viewer profile and your camera (usually camera 0).
5. Open your presentation or PDF and start slideshow/presentation mode.
6. Press Ctrl+Alt+P to resume gestures, then click the slide or PDF page.

GESTURES
Show ONE OPEN PALM, with all four fingers straight. Hold it still briefly
until tracking is ready, then move horizontally in the mirrored preview:

  SWIPE LEFT  = NEXT slide, animation step, or PDF page
  SWIPE RIGHT = PREVIOUS slide, animation step, or PDF page

Each swipe sends one command. Wait for the 0.8-second cooldown and hold your
palm still before swiping again. Close your palm while repositioning it.
Use Reverse directions to swap the mapping. High sensitivity accepts smaller
swipes; Low sensitivity requires larger swipes. Settings changes pause gestures.

KEYBOARD CONTROLS
P = pause/resume while the app has focus
Q = quit while the app has focus
Ctrl+Alt+P = pause/resume globally, including while the app is minimized
Ctrl+Alt+Q = quit globally and release the camera
The app also has Pause/Resume and Quit buttons.

VIEWER PROFILES
PowerPoint / Google Slides: normal next/previous steps, including animations.
Adobe Acrobat / Reader: next/previous PDF page.
SumatraPDF: next/previous page; use single-page or presentation mode.
Click the actual slide or PDF area. Commands go to the focused window.
Browser PDF viewers are outside guaranteed first-release compatibility.

IF IT DOES NOT WORK
No camera: close camera/video-call apps, check Windows camera permissions,
or select another camera index and click Apply camera / Retry camera.
No navigation: resume gestures, select the matching viewer profile, and
click your slide/PDF. Navigation is suppressed while PPhandcontrol has focus.
Poor tracking: use even lighting and keep the whole hand visible.
If a global shortcut is unavailable, use P/Q in the app or its buttons.
The EXE is unsigned. If device policy blocks it, contact the device
administrator or follow the repository's instructions for running from source.
